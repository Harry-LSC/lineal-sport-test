About The Yusuf Pathan Plate
Named after Yusuf Pathan, Player of the Match in the 2008 IPL Final. The Yusuf Pathan Plate is seeded with the winner of the first IPL tournament: Rajasthan Royals, who beat Chennai Super Kings on 1 June 2008.
