The rules in 20 seconds
Seed
The winner of the first official match becomes the seed champion. For club or league competitions, the winner of the inaugural tournament or season is the seed champion!
Transfer
Beat the reigning champion in an official match and the championship transfers.
Defence
Win, draw or tie and the reigning champion records a defence, where that result exists in the sport or competition.
Names
Each championship is given its own identity, usually named after a standout figure connected with its inaugural event.
Dormancy
Inactivity does not strip the title. The championship remains with its holder until an eligible defeat transfers it.
