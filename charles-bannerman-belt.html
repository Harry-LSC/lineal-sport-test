Featured Turning Point
An earlier version of the Charles Bannerman Belt lineage treated South Africa as continuing to hold the title throughout its period of international isolation, which meant the belt was shown as changing hands in its first Test after readmission in 1992. Readers correctly pointed out that this was inconsistent with the project’s dormancy principle. The lineage has therefore been amended so that, following South Africa’s final Test in March 1970, the belt reverts to Australia rather than remaining dormant with South Africa.

About The Charles Bannerman Belt
Named for Charles Bannerman, whose 165 retired hurt for Australia in the very first Test match remains the standout individual performance of that fixture. Seeded with Australia's win over England in the first-ever Test match, completed on 19 March 1877 at Melbourne. Scope: Test-playing nations, as each gained Test status over time (South Africa 1889, West Indies 1928, New Zealand 1930, India 1932, Pakistan 1952, Sri Lanka 1982, Zimbabwe 1992, Bangladesh 2000, Ireland and Afghanistan 2018). Data compiled from Wikipedia's season-by-season "International cricket in [year]" archives, cross-checked series by series — full match-by-match detail wherever the reigning champion had at least one loss in a series, series-level results where they didn't lose at all.
