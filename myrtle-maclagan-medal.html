About The Myrtle Maclagan Medal
Named after Myrtle Maclagan, who scored 72 and took 7 for 10 in the very first women's Test match, then made the format's first-ever century in the second Test of that same 1934–35 tour. Seeded with England's win over Australia in the first-ever women's Test match, played 28–31 December 1934 at Brisbane and dated to the match's final day. England and Australia traded the Medal between themselves for 70 years, until India beat England at Taunton on 1 September 2006 to capture it. India then held the Medal for almost 20 years, defending it against England, Australia and South Africa before Australia ended the run at the WACA in March 2026.
