Method

  • Seed: international lineages begin with the winner of the first official match in that format. The IPL lineage begins with the winner of the inaugural tournament.
  • Transfer: beat the reigning holder in an official match and the championship changes hands. A draw, tie or no result leaves the holder unchanged unless an official tiebreak produces a winner.
  • Test dates: a Test reign begins on the date the match is completed, not the first day of the fixture.
  • Same-day matches: where more than one eligible match is played on the same date, official match numbering determines chronological order.
  • Eligibility: tournament and bilateral matches count equally. Associate nations are eligible whenever the match itself has official international status.
  • Dormancy: inactivity does not strip the title. The championship remains with its holder until an eligible defeat transfers it.
Data & transparency

Independently reconstructed.

Current results and fixtures are checked against official competition, ICC and national-board records. Historical lineages are reconstructed from match records and cross-checked against reputable score archives, including ACS Cricket Archive and Cricsheet where appropriate.

These lineages are our own reconstructions rather than official championships. Where a result, date or other historical detail cannot be verified reliably, it is left unverified rather than inferred.
