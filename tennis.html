Method

  • Seed: each lineage begins from its audited Open Era starting point and then follows the championship through each eligible title match.
  • Transfer: beat the reigning holder in a played match and the championship changes hands. A new champion begins with zero successful defences.
  • Defence: when the reigning holder wins a played title match, the reign continues and one defence is added.
  • Walkovers: do not transfer the championship and do not count as defences or played title matches.
  • Retirements and defaults: a played match can transfer the championship when the official result records the holder as losing.
  • Representation: player country and flag data follow the recorded historical representation used in the publication data.
Data & transparency

Independently reconstructed.

The men’s and women’s Open Era lineages were reconstructed and audited match by match. Current publication data is maintained through the LSC Tennis Update System.

These are independent lineal championships rather than official ATP or WTA titles. Dates, surfaces, results and player representation are published from the audited record rather than inferred when uncertain.
