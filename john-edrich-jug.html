About The John Edrich Jug
Named after John Edrich, who top-scored with 82 and won the first-ever Man of the Match award in ODI history — in a match his own England side lost. Seeded with Australia's win over England in the first-ever ODI, 5 January 1971 at Melbourne — a rain-shortened substitute fixture arranged after an Ashes Test was washed out, which accidentally invented the format. Any ODI-status nation can hold the Jug, full member or associate. Data compiled from a complete, user-supplied match-by-match dataset sourced from ESPNcricinfo, including the official ODI number for every match.
