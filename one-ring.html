The strange statistic
Nobody held it longest.

The first vacancy lasted about 2,461 years — roughly half of the Ring's entire existence and longer than Sauron's own possession.

What counts as possession?

Independent custody, not mere touch.

Sam counts because he takes custody, carries the Ring independently and wears it. Gandalf and Tom Bombadil do not create new lineage periods because their handling is brief and another person's custody clearly continues.

Sources & method

A serious chronology first; the lineage-tracker format comes second.

Primary sources. The Lord of the Rings, especially Appendix B; The Fellowship of the Ring (“The Shadow of the Past”, “A Long-expected Party”); The Two Towers (“The Choices of Master Samwise”); The Return of the King (“The Tower of Cirith Ungol”, “Mount Doom”); Unfinished Tales (“The Disaster of the Gladden Fields”); The Silmarillion (“Of the Rings of Power and the Third Age”); and The Hobbit (“Riddles in the Dark”).

Possession rule. The tracker records independent physical custody. Brief handling or examination does not create a new possession period if another individual's custody clearly continues.

Approximate dates. Where Tolkien supplies only a year or approximate period, this tracker keeps that uncertainty rather than inventing a month or day.

Vacancy. A Ring lying lost and unheld is treated as vacant. Rivers, tunnels and places are not personified as bearers.

Destruction. Destruction is a terminal event, not a transfer to a new possessor.
